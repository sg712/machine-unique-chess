# Bibliography and research foundations

Updated 16 September 2026. These sources motivate the project or supply its instruments. Their results are not evidence that this trainer improves chess strength. The [dated literature audit](docs/RESEARCH_LITERATURE_2026-09-16.md) records study populations, contrary evidence, access limitations, and concrete next experiments.

- **Schut, Tomašev, McGrath, Hassabis, Paquet and Kim.** *Bridging the human–AI knowledge gap through concept discovery and transfer in AlphaZero.* PNAS 122(13), e2406675122, 2025. [Journal paper](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2406675122), [2023 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.16410). Four grandmasters improved on concept prototypes. Our different Leela procedure has not replicated that human result.
- **Tang et al.** *Maia-2: A Unified Model for Human-AI Alignment in Chess.* NeurIPS 2024. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.20553), [code](https://github.com/CSSLab/maia2). Supplies the skill-conditioned human-move model used by the main mining pipeline. Our four tested rating settings are an experimental choice, not a continuous evaluation of every rating.
- **Monroe, Eilender, Chalmers, Tang and Anderson.** *Chessformer: A Unified Architecture for Chess Modeling.* ICLR 2026; preprint 18 May 2026. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19091), [official Maia-3 code](https://github.com/CSSLab/maia3). Historical FEN-only ranking and later real-history comparisons remain separate experiments; neither changes the original 5,155-position definition.
- **Jenner et al.** *Evidence of Learned Look-Ahead in a Chess-Playing Neural Network.* [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.00877), [interpretability code](https://github.com/HumanCompatibleAI/leela-interp). Provides background and open tooling for inspecting Leela representations. The present clustering analysis is not an activation-level causal replication.
- **Stockfish developers.** [Stockfish source](https://github.com/official-stockfish/Stockfish), [Stockfish 18 release](https://stockfishchess.org/blog/2026/stockfish-18/). Historical analyses use 17.1; the version-2 pilot pins 18. Finite-depth evaluations are estimates, not exhaustive ground truth. [WDL documentation](https://github.com/official-stockfish/WDL_model) describes engine self-play calibration, not human win probability.
- **Inamdar, Tang, Anderson and Zemel.** *Level Up: Defining and Exploiting Transitional Problems for Curriculum Learning.* [Preprint, revised 3 June 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13761). Motivates a separate transitional-position curriculum. Its learning experiments train models, not human students.
- **Lin et al.** *Tracing the Thought of a Grandmaster-level Chess-Playing Transformer.* [Preprint, 11 April 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.10158). Supplies a possible sparse/causal LC0 research direction; this project has not performed those interventions.
- **Kim, Goh, Hwang, Cho and Ok.** *Bridging the Gap between Expert and Language Models: Concept-guided Chess Commentary Generation and Evaluation.* [NAACL 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.naacl-long.481/). Commentary ratings and residual factual errors motivate independent explanation auditing; they are not teaching-efficacy results.
- **Tang et al.** *Grounded Chess Reasoning in Language Models via Master Distillation.* [Preprint, 20 March 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20510). Introduces C1. Puzzle-solving accuracy does not certify explanation faithfulness or human learning.
- **Poulidis, Hamsa Bastani and Osbert Bastani.** *Self-Regulated AI Use Hinders Long-Term Learning.* [Working paper, revised 1 August 2026](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5604932). Direct chess-learning evidence relevant to assistance policy; this audit verified the primary abstract, not the full methods.
- **Poulidis, Ge, Hamsa Bastani and Osbert Bastani.** *Action vs. Attention Signals for Human-AI Collaboration: Evidence from Chess.* [Working paper, revised 10 July 2025](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5128584). Motivates studying decisions after a hint or answer. Primary-abstract access only in this audit.
- **Johnson.** *ChessMimic: Per-Rating Transformer Models for Human Move, Clock, and Outcome Prediction in Online Blitz Chess.* [Preprint, 3 June 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.04473). A history/clock-aware behavioral comparator; blitz results do not validate untimed puzzle probabilities.
- **Lomasov et al.** *Exploring Human-AI Conceptual Alignment through the Prism of Chess.* [Preprint, 29 October 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.26025). Provides concept-probe distribution-shift tests. Probe failure alone does not prove novel knowledge or memorization.
- **Leela Chess Zero contributors.** [Project](https://lczero.org/). The project uses an open network through the interpretability tooling; historical checkpoint provenance should be made more reproducible before a confirmatory study.
- **Lichess contributors.** [Game and puzzle databases](https://database.lichess.org/). Public games supply source positions and actual moves; puzzle tags supply the selected motif basis.
- **Lichess Elite Database.** [Archive](https://database.nikonoel.fr/). Supplies filtered elite games. Its time-control and date distribution differ from the club sample.

Earlier speculative literature notes and unverified product comparisons are retained in Git history. The current research claims and their limitations are documented in [methods](docs/METHODS.md).
