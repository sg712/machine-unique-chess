Why these positions?

I’m studying moves that Stockfish likes but Maia, a model of human play, is unlikely to choose. I analysed 123,405 positions and selected 5,155 that met this filter. The trainer uses 320 of them, organised into eight groups.

The question is whether practicing them helps us find similar moves in our own games. I haven’t tested that with a controlled study yet. For now, you can try the positions and read what I’ve found so far.
