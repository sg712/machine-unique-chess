Why these positions?

I’m studying moves that Stockfish likes but Maia, a model of human play, is unlikely to choose. The original collection contained 123,405 Black-to-move positions; 5,155 met that first filter. The trainer uses 320 of those positions, organised into eight groups.

The research now includes both colours. Read about the expanded collection and its checks.

The question is whether practicing them helps us find similar moves in our own games. I haven’t tested that with a controlled study yet. For now, you can try the positions and read what I’ve found so far.

Take the twelve-position test if you’d like to try a longer set.
