The percentage is how often players who actually reached these positions over the board found the engine's move. Lower means fewer found it in the source games.

Where this comes from

Every position comes from a real game. A position qualifies when Stockfish at depth 16 rates its chosen move at least 100 centipawns above Maia-2's favourite at 2000, while Maia-2 assigns the engine move at most 5% probability at 1100, 1400, 1700 and 2000. Of 123,405 positions analysed, 5,155 qualified; in 3,956 the player chose a different move. Another move can still be good. Exact engine agreement is not the same as move quality.

The eight groups above come from clustering the first 1,745 of those by how the engine represents them internally. The full method and findings →
