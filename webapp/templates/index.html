Can a person learn what only the engine knows?

Chess engines prefer moves that essentially no human plays — at any rating. This study tests whether those moves can be taught the way DeepMind taught them to four grandmasters: by silent example, with no explanation at all.

Reference rates

On these positions, simulated humans find the engine's move under 3% of the time at every rating from 1100 to 2600. Real players over the board manage 13% around 2000, and 47% at 2600+. The grandmaster in the original study scored 0/4 before study and 42% after.
