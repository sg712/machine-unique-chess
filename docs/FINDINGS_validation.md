# Grouping and concept evidence

Updated 7 September 2026. Generated by `scripts/render_research_notes.py` from the linked result JSON. Older snapshots and superseded interpretations remain in Git history.

On 1,745 selected embeddings, the k=8 silhouette is 0.0715, against 0.0078 after independently shuffling each dimension. Absolute separation is small. This null destroys correlations, not just potential chess categories.

Resampling stability (mean adjusted Rand index): k=6 0.904; k=8 0.800. Eight groups are a teaching choice, not a discovered natural count. Alternative methods give different partitions and many noise points.

The k=8 external permutation test gives p=0.009; k=9 gives p=0.063. The p-value is a tail frequency under the tested null, not a probability that a hypothesis is true. Several choices of k were explored without a multiple-comparison correction. Independent validation is needed.

Experiment 07 uses an earlier cached set of 646 selected positions and 400 controls. Twelve motif directions reconstruct the average selected-minus-control direction with R²=0.462. Mean per-position reconstruction is 0.202 versus 0.184 in controls. The residual is not evidence that half the concepts cannot be expressed in chess language.

Experiments 05/05b did not establish transferable sparse directions: none of 30 individual fits generalized; grouped fits scored 1.00 within group and 0.515 held out. The representation, rollouts and evaluation differ from Schut et al. The later groups are an exploratory way to organize practice. No completed controlled study establishes their teaching effectiveness.

Experiment 19's stronger representation interpretation is superseded by the controlled comparison and foldwise PCA audit in [difficulty findings](FINDINGS_difficulty.md).
