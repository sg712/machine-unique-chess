## Short invitation

I’m looking for adult chess players to help test Machine Unique Chess, a project about learning from engine moves that people often overlook.

You’ll practise a set of examples and solve some new positions. We’re comparing two ways of ordering the same practice material. We don’t yet know whether the ordering improves learning.

You’re eligible if you are 18 or older, have an established Lichess rapid rating of 1800–2600, and haven’t used the Machine Unique Chess trainer or read its worked examples. The first appointment takes about 45–55 minutes, followed by a 10–15 minute appointment roughly a week later. Participation is voluntary, and you can stop at any time.
