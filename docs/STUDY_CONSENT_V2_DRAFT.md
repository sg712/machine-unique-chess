## About this chess-learning study

Machine Unique Chess is testing whether studying related chess positions together helps people recognize the same ideas in new positions. You would receive either grouped examples or the same examples in a shuffled order. Assignment is random. Both versions use the same positions, explanations and amount of practice.

We do not yet know whether either version helps people improve. This study will not estimate an Elo gain or provide an official rating.

## Who can take part

You must be at least 18, have an established Lichess rapid rating between 1800 and 2600, and have no previous exposure to these training materials. We will ask about your rating, approximate number of rated games and any positions you recognize. Please tell the researcher if you have used the Machine Unique Chess trainer or read its worked examples.

## What you would do

There are two appointments. The first is expected to take about 45–55 minutes with the initial eight-family material bank. You will solve eight positions, practise 24 examples, and solve eight different positions. Practice gives you 30 seconds to choose a move and 30 seconds to read the explanation. Assessments allow 60 seconds per position. The researcher will explain the controls before the first assessment.

A second appointment, about 10–15 minutes long, takes place seven days later, with a permitted window from day five to day nine. You will solve eight further positions and tell us about any chess practice between appointments. We will offer the assessment explanations after that appointment or if you withdraw.

Please work without an engine, another board or outside assistance during the assessments. Tell us about interruptions or technical problems. A timer can make chess questions feel frustrating; this is a test of the study materials, and you can stop at any point.

## Your choice to participate

Participation is voluntary. You may decline any question or stop without giving a reason. This does not affect your access to the ordinary trainer or any relationship with the researcher. The researcher will explain how to withdraw and how to request deletion of identifiable study records.

## What information would be recorded

The study would record a randomly assigned participant code; rating information and prior exposure; positions shown; chosen chess moves; response times and timeouts; practice order; explanation and replay use; interruptions; completion or withdrawal; and the timing of the second appointment. We would keep your original moves as well as derived scores.
